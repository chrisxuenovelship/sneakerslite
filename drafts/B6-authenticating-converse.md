A canvas upper and rubber toe cap can make Converse look straightforward to authenticate. For a resale platform, the challenge is deciding which details match the claimed model and release, consistently across a large intake of listings.

## Why Converse Is Targeted

A general-release pair can have a low unit price, but high trading volume makes a familiar silhouette worth copying. Manual inspection that makes sense for an expensive collaboration can consume too much margin on an everyday pair. Both need checking, but not the same risk policy.

**Chuck 70 is not the Chuck Taylor All Star.** They share an outline, but the 70 typically has a higher rubber sidewall, glossier foxing and more substantial build; the All Star tends towards a flatter, more matte presentation. Mixing their reference images can make authentic differences look suspicious.

**The catalogue extends beyond Chucks.** One Star has different panel geometry and a different set of material and label comparisons. Run Star Hike and Run Star Motion add prominent sculpted sole construction, making shape and proportion especially important. These are distinct inspection tasks, not variants of a single canvas-shoe template.

**Collaborations change the stakes.** CDG PLAY, Off-White, Stüssy and Rick Owens DRKSHDW releases can carry meaningful resale value. That does not mean every pair is valuable or every release is covered. Separate the routine general-release queue from cases where collaboration details and dispute cost warrant closer review.

## Where the Tells Are

No single cue proves authenticity. Converse manufacturing changes by model, production run and factory, so the useful question is whether several details agree with the claimed pair and with one another.

**Construction.** Compare ankle patch print alignment and the star's proportions against the correct release. Check heel licence plate placement and the stitching around it. Toe cap height and rubber texture should make sense alongside the foxing tape's thickness. On a Chuck 70, the commonly seen two-line foxing stripe and higher, glossier rubber sidewall contrast with the All Star's typically one-line stripe and more matte finish; confirm the release before treating either as a rule. Count eyelets against the size and model reference, and examine their metal finish. The vulcanised seam line should also sit coherently around the shoe, rather than changing character from one view to the next.

**Labels and packaging.** Tongue label typography and sizing format, inner-sole print, and the box label's product code and factory code provide independent comparisons. On a collaboration, a hangtag may be another reference point, but its presence alone establishes nothing. A country-of-origin line is not a universal shortcut: 'Made in' details can vary by factory and production period. Packaging may also be missing or mismatched on resale, which should be recorded as a limitation rather than silently substituted with another pair's box data.

**Materials.** Canvas weave weight and twill direction are useful only against the right model and material specification. A Chuck 70's cushioned, often OrthoLite-branded insole calls for a different comparison from a standard All Star's. Rubber gloss and texture, together with the finish of the metal eyelets, can corroborate what construction and labels suggest. Smell may prompt a human inspector to look closer, but photographs cannot verify it; it should not be presented as a machine-checked signal.

Collect side profiles, heel and toe views, labels and packaging where available. Missing views narrow what can be assessed, especially for a claimed collaboration.

## What Our Pipeline Checks

SneakersLite's pipeline begins with image standardisation. Pose estimation and OpenCV normalisation bring submitted views into comparable position and scale. For Converse, that matters when comparing toe cap height, heel placement and the relative height of a Chuck 70 sidewall: a tilted photograph can make a normal proportion look wrong.

Visual feature analysis examines colour, texture and shape ratio. It compares the silhouette of a One Star or Run Star sole, foxing and canvas, and patch or toe cap proportions where images support that comparison. It cannot infer rubber smell or inspect a missing heel view.

Tag and label authentication uses OCR on typography, barcodes and QR codes where present. Tongue and box labels give it text and code formats to compare with the claimed model. These signals are combined into a confidence score with a verification breakdown. Below-threshold cases route to human review; reviewed cases feed back into training. The output should let a platform see which evidence supported a result and where the evidence was weak, rather than treating every submission as a forced yes or no.

## Coverage Today Is a Model Question

Coverage is a model question, not a brand name on a vendor list. Chuck 70 and Chuck Taylor All Star may dominate routine catalogue questions, while One Star, Run Star Hike, Run Star Motion and collaborations may concentrate demand in other resale segments. This describes market demand, not coverage of every model or colourway named here.

Our current Converse model list is disclosed on request and expands monthly. Ask which exact models are supported and which submissions begin at a conservative confidence threshold. New models launch conservatively; as reviewed submissions add evidence over weeks, the share needing human review falls. A platform decides what to accept automatically and what requires an inspector. ## What Operators Should Ask

**Can the workflow separate everyday pairs from collaborations?** Apply the same evidence discipline to both, but set a review policy that reflects the value of a disputed pair. A collaboration with uncertain labels or incomplete accessories should not inherit the automated path established for a well-documented general release.

**What does each check cost at your mix of volume and risk?** High-volume, low-value Converse make per-item authentication cost a deciding factor. **What happens when the photographs are incomplete or the result is uncertain?** Ask to see the confidence score and verification breakdown, define who handles below-threshold cases, and decide how sellers supply missing views. The important operational measure is not a promise to eliminate review, but a review path that staff can actually run.
